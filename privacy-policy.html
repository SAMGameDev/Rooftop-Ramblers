Who we are

Your Game Name (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile endless runner game made by Your Name, an independent game developer.

This policy explains in simple words what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

What the game stores

The game keeps some information so it can remember how you play. This information is saved on your own device:

Game progress
  • Levels you unlocked and stars you earned
  • Characters you unlocked and the one you picked
  • Your Cash, ATM cards, player level and XP
  • Daily mission progress and achievements (when these features are added)
Your settings
  • Music and sound effects volume
  • Graphics quality (Low, Medium or High)

Device information. To pick good graphics settings the first time you play, the game checks basic things like your phone’s memory (RAM), processor and graphics chip. This is used only to make the game run smoothly. It is not sent to us.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine and is distributed through app stores like Google Play. These companies may collect basic technical information (for example device model, operating system, crash reports and download numbers) under their own privacy policies.

Some features we plan to add, or may add, use services from other companies:

  • Google Play Games for achievements. If you sign in, Google handles your sign-in and shares your player name and achievement progress with the game.
  • Cloud Save (planned). If we add it, your progress will be stored on a server so you can move to a new phone.
  • Ads (maybe in the future). If we show ads, the ad company may use your device’s advertising ID to show ads and measure them. You can reset or turn off this ID in your phone’s settings.

Before any of these go live in the final game, we will update this policy so it clearly matches what the game really does.

Useful links: Google Privacy Policy · Unity Privacy Policy

How we use information

We only use information to:

  • Save and load your progress
  • Keep your settings the way you like them
  • Make the game run well on your phone
  • Fix bugs and improve the game
  • Answer you if you contact us

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Kids and families

The game is made for a general audience and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone’s settings. This removes your saved progress from your device.
  • Change settings: adjust sound and graphics any time in the in-game Settings panel.
  • Ads and tracking: you can limit ad personalization in your phone’s Google or Apple settings.
  • Questions or requests: email us and we will help.

Because progress is stored on your device, uninstalling the game means you lose your progress unless Cloud Save is available and turned on.

Keeping things safe

We keep the information the game uses to a minimum, and most of it never leaves your phone. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, but we do our best to protect what little we handle.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Please check back now and then.
